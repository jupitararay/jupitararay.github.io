### At Penn State University
#### <span style="font-family: 'FiraSans', sans-serif;">Bilingualism - Spring 2027 (Co-teaching with Dr Deborah Morton)</span>
This course will explore the socio- and psycholinguistic consequences of bilingualism. Topics include languages in contact, 
interference, attrition, bilingual memory, and bilingual grammars.

### At Boston University
#### <span style="font-family: 'FiraSans', sans-serif;">Introduction to Sound System - Fall 2023 (TF for Dr Kate Lindsey)</span>
This course addressed topics on articulatory and acoustic phonetics,  basic phonological analysis, 
and focused on cross-language typology and comparison. Students received hands-on experience with phonetic analysis, 
including IPA transcription, field techniques, and digital speech analysis.

#### <span style="font-family: 'FiraSans', sans-serif;">Introduction to Linguistics - Fall 2021 & Fall 2022 (TF for Dr Kate Lindsey, Dr Jon Barnes)</span>
This course provided a foundation in linguistics by exploring shared properties and structural differences across human languages. 
Key topics include phonetics, morphology, syntax, semantics, language acquisition, historical change, sociolinguistic variation, 
across spoken, written, and signed modalities.
