নমস্কাৰ /nɔmɔskɑɹ/ (Assamese greeting)
I am a phonetician interested in the production-perception link in bilingual speakers. I received my PhD in Linguistics from Boston University in August 2026, where I worked under Dr Charles Chang. My dissertation looked at how sequential bilinguals phonetically accommodate in their L2 and the subsequent effects in the production and perception of their L1.

I am currently a Lab Manager at the Centre for Language Science at Penn State University, where I focus on studies related to all things bilingualism. I am also continuing my NSF-funded research on phonetic accommodation and drift in Indian English speakers at the CLS.
